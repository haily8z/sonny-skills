# Giọng văn (Style Definitions) — nội bộ, KHÔNG hiển thị cho user

File này chứa prompt nội bộ cho từng giọng văn. KHÔNG hiển thị nội dung prompt cho user. Chỉ nạp style tương ứng vào prompt sinh content khi viết.

---

## STYLE 1: Triết gia (Philosopher)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content sản phẩm/dịch vụ cao cấp, mang tính nghệ thuật, hoặc khi muốn tạo chiều sâu cảm xúc đặc biệt.

**Style prompt (nạp vào cuối prompt framework):**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Author] = Philosopher, poet, or literary thinker
[Professional title] = Writer, philosopher, poet
[Content type] = Emotional literary prose or essay
[Directness level] = Indirect, symbolic, and metaphorical language
[Humor level] = None, focuses on pain, deep emotions, and the nature of art
[Educational level] = High, requires understanding of philosophy and literature
[Data-Driven level] = Low, based on emotions and personal viewpoints rather than data
[Intensity level] = High, emotionally charged, portraying inner turmoil and artistic struggle
[Tone] = Serious, deep, philosophical, and slightly melancholic.
```

## STYLE 2: Thân thiện & Trò chuyện (Friendly & Conversational)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content hướng đến đối tượng đại chúng, muốn tạo cảm giác gần gũi, dễ tiếp cận. Phù hợp với hầu hết sản phẩm B2C.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Friendly, casual, and approachable. Voice is conversational, warm, and engaging.
[Sentence Structure] = Short to medium length, often including questions to engage the reader, with informal phrasing, contractions, and direct address like "you" or "we."
[Vocabulary] = Simple and familiar, avoiding jargon or overly technical terms, may include slang or colloquial expressions appropriate to the audience.
[Rhetorical Devices] = Humor, anecdotes, and rhetorical questions to maintain a lively and relatable tone.
[Pacing] = Smooth and interactive, presenting ideas in a logical yet informal sequence with short, snappy, and easily scannable paragraphs.
[Overall] = Relatable, personal, and engaging, using conversational hooks to maintain interest.
```

---

## STYLE 3: Chuyên nghiệp & Uy tín (Professional & Authoritative)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content B2B, dịch vụ tư vấn, tài chính, bất động sản cao cấp, hoặc khi cần xây dựng lòng tin và sự uy tín.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Formal and respectful. Voice is authoritative, confident, and knowledgeable.
[Sentence Structure] = Medium to long, often complex or compound for clarity, using parallel structures for emphasis.
[Vocabulary] = Advanced but accessible, avoiding overly technical jargon unless necessary, with polished and precise word choice.
[Rhetorical Devices] = Facts, statistics, and logical appeals (logos) to reinforce authority, with minimal figurative language.
[Pacing] = Deliberate and measured, with clear transitions and balanced, well-structured paragraphs featuring topic sentences.
[Overall] = Instructive, credible, and poised, focusing on delivering value and building trust.
```

---

## STYLE 4: Truyền cảm hứng & Động viên (Inspirational & Motivational)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content khoá học, coaching, phát triển bản thân, fitness, hoặc khi muốn thúc đẩy hành động thông qua cảm hứng.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Positive, uplifting, and empowering. Voice is passionate, encouraging, and optimistic.
[Sentence Structure] = Mix of short and long for dramatic effect, often using imperative sentences to inspire action.
[Vocabulary] = Simple to intermediate, with emotionally charged words, action verbs, and phrases like "You can," "Achieve," and "Believe."
[Rhetorical Devices] = Metaphors, analogies, and alliteration to create impact, along with emotional appeals (pathos) to stir excitement and hope.
[Pacing] = Dynamic and energetic, with pauses for emphasis and short bursts of inspiring ideas.
[Overall] = Empowering, vivid, and enthusiastic, encouraging the reader to believe in their potential.
```

## STYLE 5: Giáo dục & Thông tin (Informative & Educational)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content sản phẩm kỹ thuật, phần mềm, dịch vụ chuyên ngành, hoặc khi khách hàng cần hiểu rõ trước khi mua.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Neutral and informative. Voice is professional, clear, and instructive.
[Sentence Structure] = Medium to long, prioritizing clarity, using structured information presentation.
[Vocabulary] = Intermediate to advanced depending on subject matter, including technical terms with clear explanations.
[Rhetorical Devices] = Analogies and examples to simplify complex concepts, maintaining an objective and factual style.
[Pacing] = Logical and well-organized, with compact paragraphs focused on single ideas.
[Overall] = Straightforward, detailed, and instructive, aiming to deliver clear value.
```

---

## STYLE 6: Hài hước & Dí dỏm (Humorous & Witty)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content sản phẩm lifestyle, F&B, giải trí, hoặc thương hiệu có tính cách vui vẻ, trẻ trung.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Lighthearted, playful, and funny. Voice is clever, sarcastic, or whimsical.
[Sentence Structure] = Short and punchy for comedic timing, often using unexpected twists or parenthetical side comments.
[Vocabulary] = Simple to intermediate, with playful twists and possible use of puns or pop culture references.
[Rhetorical Devices] = Hyperbole, irony, and wordplay to entertain while making a point.
[Pacing] = Fast-paced with surprising turns, compact paragraphs to keep the humor fresh.
[Overall] = Entertaining, sharp, and memorable, keeping readers engaged and amused.
```

---

## STYLE 7: Cảm xúc & Gợi mở (Emotional & Evocative)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content sản phẩm liên quan đến gia đình, sức khoẻ, kỷ niệm, hoặc khi muốn chạm vào cảm xúc sâu của khách hàng.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Deep, moving, and heartfelt. Voice is personal, empathetic, and reflective.
[Sentence Structure] = Varies between short emotional bursts and longer reflective sentences, often using pauses or ellipses for emphasis.
[Vocabulary] = Simple but emotionally charged, with words that evoke strong feelings and vivid imagery.
[Rhetorical Devices] = Similes, metaphors, and vivid descriptions to appeal to senses and emotions.
[Pacing] = Fluid and lyrical, with intentional breaks. Alternates between short impactful statements and immersive storytelling.
[Overall] = Poignant, vivid, and heartfelt, creating strong emotional connections.
```

---

## STYLE 8: Thuyết phục & Bán hàng (Persuasive & Sales-Oriented)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content chạy ads trực tiếp, flash sale, khuyến mãi, hoặc khi mục tiêu chính là chuyển đổi nhanh.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Persuasive and enthusiastic. Voice is confident, convincing, and benefit-driven.
[Sentence Structure] = Short to medium, designed to drive urgency, often using repetition and calls to action for emphasis.
[Vocabulary] = Simple and benefit-focused, featuring power words like "Exclusive," "Save," and "Proven."
[Rhetorical Devices] = Testimonials, scarcity, and social proof to appeal to both logic and emotion.
[Pacing] = Fast-paced and action-oriented, with short paragraphs guiding readers toward a specific goal.
[Overall] = Compelling, energetic, and result-driven, motivating readers to take action.
```

---

## STYLE 9: Suy tư & Triết lý (Thought-Provoking & Philosophical)

**Khi nào dùng:** Content thương hiệu cao cấp, sản phẩm nghệ thuật, hoặc khi muốn tạo sự khác biệt bằng chiều sâu tư duy.

**Style prompt:**

```
Adopt the following writing style for the entire content:
[Tone and Voice] = Reflective, profound, and contemplative. Voice is intellectual, abstract, and poetic.
[Sentence Structure] = Long and complex, with reflective pauses, often featuring parallelism and rhetorical questions.
[Vocabulary] = Advanced and abstract, with metaphorical and nuanced language.
[Rhetorical Devices] = Philosophical analogies, paradoxes, and metaphors to challenge the reader's thinking.
[Pacing] = Slow, deliberate, and introspective, with longer paragraphs developing profound ideas.
[Overall] = Intellectual, rich, and abstract, prompting deep reflection and thought.
```

Giọng văn được giữ nguyên xuyên suốt bài. Nếu user muốn đổi giọng văn, chỉ cần nói và Claude sẽ viết lại với style mới.
